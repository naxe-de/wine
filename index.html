Wine

Biodynamic grower Maria Thun found that wine tastes different depending on where the Moon stands among the constellations. Fruit and flower days are good for wine. Root and leaf days are not. The hours around the Moon crossing a node or passing perigee (six either side) are generally unfavourable whatever the constellation, and show grey on the dial.

Red on the dial means yes. Grey means no. Drag the bottle around the dial to check any moment.

Where you are does not matter: the Moon stands in the same constellation for everyone on Earth at the same instant. Only the clock time differs, so set your time zone in settings and the dial is right for you.

This page computes the Moon's position itself (Meeus) and uses the astronomical constellation boundaries. The Thun calendar's exact boundaries are unpublished, so a change can differ from the printed book by a few hours. Student project, built to understand how the Thun wine calendar works.
